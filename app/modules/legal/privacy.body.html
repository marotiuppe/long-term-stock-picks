3. What we do not collect

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking scripts, and no tracking pixels. We keep only an anonymous count of page views (section 2), with nothing that identifies you.
  • No email address, phone number, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details or demat account number.
  • No broker passwords or PINs. Please never type one into this app. The only broker credential it accepts is a Groww API key and secret that you choose to give it.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to show you adverts.

4. Why we use it

  • To run the app for you: to show your portfolio, notes and alerts, and to keep them between visits and across devices once you have an account.
  • To keep your account secure: to check your password, keep you signed in, and slow down guessing of passwords.
  • To protect the service: to stop abuse and to find and fix faults.
  • To see how much the site is used: from the anonymous page-view totals, which cannot be traced to anyone.
  • To meet legal duties: where the law requires it.

We rely on your consent, which you give by using the app, entering data or creating an account. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data (section 8). Withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide the parts of the app that need that data.

6. Where it is kept

Your data is kept on our hosting and database providers' servers. Those servers may be outside India, and using the app means your data may be processed there.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your data stays until you delete it. We do not currently delete inactive guest data automatically, so please delete data you no longer want kept.
  • The guest cookie lasts one year. A sign-in session lasts 30 days, after which you must sign in again.
  • When you use Delete my data, everything linked to your guest ID or account is removed from our database, including your account, sessions and any saved Groww credentials. Backups kept by our database provider may hold it for a short while longer before they are overwritten.

8. Your rights and choices

You have the right to know what we hold, to correct it, to have it deleted, to withdraw consent, to nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you cannot, and to complain. You can do most of this yourself:

  • See and download your data: Settings or Portfolio, then Export everything (JSON) or Export transactions (CSV).
  • Correct it: edit your holdings, notes and settings in the app. For your username or password, use the Account page.
  • Delete it: Portfolio, then Delete my data. It asks you to confirm first. This removes your holdings, notes, alerts, settings, account and sessions.
  • Remove your Groww key: on the Portfolio page, in the Groww sync card, choose Forget saved key. This deletes the stored key and secret. To stop using Groww to sign in, use Unlink Groww on the Account page.
  • Anything else: email {{email}}. We will reply within a reasonable time and within any period the law sets. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the rights under GDPR or UK GDPR that apply to you, including to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Security

  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes.
  • Sign-in tokens are random, and only their hash is stored, so a copy of the database cannot be used to sign in.
  • Groww keys and secrets are encrypted before they are stored. Your Groww user ID is stored only as a keyed hash.
  • Cookies are marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, and Secure on the live site, and the live site uses HTTPS.
  • Repeated sign-in attempts are slowed down.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise it. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires. Keep your password private and use one you use nowhere else.

About Groww keys. A Groww API key may be allowed to trade, not just read. This app only reads holdings, but if you would rather not store a key with us, use manual entry or a CSV import instead, and choose Forget saved key at any time.
