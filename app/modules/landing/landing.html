How it works

Three steps, repeated every trading day.

  1. 1

    Scan after the close

    Once the market has closed, the app downloads five years of daily prices for the liquid NSE shares and ETFs, and drops anything too thin or too jumpy to trust.

  2. 2

    Score and forecast

    Each stock gets a score. The forecast is what stocks with a similar score did next, scaled to this stock's own ups and downs, and checked on data the scoring never saw.

  3. 3

    You decide

    You get dates, a stop-loss and the reasons. You place any order yourself, with your own broker. The app never trades for you.

The honest numbers

This has no proven edge. In our own back-test, buy-grade picks rose about 57% of the time over one month, against about 56% for any stock. That gap is too small to count on.

So treat every number as a historical base rate, never as a promise of profit. The value here is a calm process: clear dates, a stop-loss decided in advance and reasons you can check. The full method and the test report are public.
