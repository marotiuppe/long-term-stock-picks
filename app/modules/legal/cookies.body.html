Cookie Policy

Short version: we use two small cookies that the app cannot work without, and a few settings saved in your browser. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept.

Cookies we set

Both are set by this site itself (first-party), are HttpOnly (page scripts cannot read them), use SameSite=Strict (they are not sent from other sites), and are marked Secure on the live site.

NameWhat it doesLastsNeeded?
uidA random ID that links your browser to your portfolio, notes and alerts, as a guest or after you sign in.1 yearYes. Without it the app cannot remember your data.
sidKeeps you signed in. Holds a random token; we store only its hash. Set only after you sign in or create an account.30 days, or until you sign outYes, for signing in.

Counting page views uses no cookie: it is a plain total per page and day (see the Privacy Policy).

Because these are strictly necessary for a service you asked for, they are set without a consent banner. They are still personal data, which is covered by the Privacy Policy.

Settings saved in your browser

The app also saves a few preferences in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are not sent to us. They remember how you like the app to look and work:

NameWhat it remembers
themeLight or dark (dark unless you choose light)
sidebarWhether the side menu is collapsed
hz, psz, denseThe time horizon you last picked, rows per page, and compact table rows
ltThe budget and maximum-loss figures you typed into the position-size tool
pcprefs, calcStateYour chart settings and the what-if calculator's last inputs
notify, notifiedWhether you turned on browser notifications, and which alerts have already been shown

Third parties

We set no third-party cookies. Pages ask Google Fonts for the Inter typeface, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. See section 5 of the Privacy Policy. Nothing else on our pages loads from another site.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you remove uid, the app will no longer connect you to your saved guest data, so export it first (Settings, then Export everything). Signing in to an account gets your data back on any browser.
  • You can clear local storage in your browser's site settings. The app then goes back to its defaults.
  • To remove your data from our servers, use Delete my data on the Portfolio page.
